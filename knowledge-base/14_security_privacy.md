# Security and Privacy

## Privacy Principle

NexGen protects private shopping, account, and order information through appropriate access and verification steps.

## Public Information

General fashion guidance, public product discovery, store discovery, and approved public information may be discussed without customer verification.

## Private Information

Verification may be required before discussing:

- Customer profiles
- Saved addresses
- Order history
- Shipment details
- Returns and refunds
- Exchanges and cancellations
- Loyalty information

## Safe Verification

A spoken name, email address, telephone number, or order reference does not by itself prove identity. Follow the available verification journey.

## Information Customers Should Not Share

Customers should not provide:

- Account passwords
- Complete payment credentials
- Payment security codes
- Unnecessary identity documents
- Codes they did not request

## Email and Links

Use only official NexGen messages and destinations. If a message appears suspicious, avoid opening it and contact support through an official route.

## Data Minimization

Ask only for information required for the current request. Avoid repeating private information aloud when a shorter confirmation is sufficient.

## Customer Boundaries

Never disclose one customer’s information to another person. When identity or authorization is uncertain, pause private assistance and offer a secure next step.
