# Orders and Shipping

## Order Assistance

Customers can ask NexGen to help with order confirmation, processing progress, packing, shipment, delivery, cancellation options, and delivery issues.

Private order information requires identity verification.

## Order Status Journey

An order may move through stages such as pending confirmation, confirmed, processing, packed, shipped, and delivered. Other outcomes may include cancellation, return, refund, or exchange.

The current status must always be checked. A general status description does not confirm the state of a particular order.

## Shipping Information

Shipping methods, destinations, estimates, charges, and restrictions can change. Ask NexGen to check the latest shipping information for the current order or destination.

An estimated delivery date is not a guaranteed arrival time unless explicitly stated by the active service.

## Tracking

Tracking may become available after a shipment is prepared. Customers may be asked to verify identity before tracking details are disclosed.

## Delivery Issues

For a delayed, missing, damaged, or incorrectly delivered package, collect the order reference and relevant facts. Avoid promising a refund or replacement before review.

## Email Notifications

Customers may receive messages when important order events occur, including confirmation, packing, shipment, delivery, return, exchange, or cancellation updates.

Customers should use official NexGen links and avoid sharing passwords or payment security codes by email.
