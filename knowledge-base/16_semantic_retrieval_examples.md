# Semantic Retrieval Examples

## Workwear Discovery

**Customer says:** “I need something for work.”

**Related wording:** office outfit, professional clothes, business casual, polished separates.

**Synonyms:** workwear, officewear, corporate dressing.

**Associated concepts:** formality, comfort, tailoring, layering, workplace dress code.

**Expected understanding:** Clarify workplace formality, preferred garment, size, color, and budget before searching.

## Wedding Outfit

**Synonyms:** occasionwear, celebration dressing, formal event outfit.

**Associated concepts:** dress code, venue, weather, time of day, footwear comfort.

**Expected understanding:** Ask for role, dress code, location, preferred silhouette, and exclusions.

## Black Dresses

**Customer says:** “Show me black dresses.”

**Related wording:** black dress, little black dress, dark evening dress.

## Spoken Corrections

**Customer says:** “No, I meant navy. Actually, make that medium.”

**Synonyms:** correction, revision, change, replacement preference, restart.

**Alternate wording:** not that one, go back, start over, cancel that, I changed my mind, show me something else, try another color.

**Common speech patterns:** “No, navy.” “I meant medium.” “Go back.” “Forget that.” “Let’s try again.”

**Expected understanding:** Update only the corrected preference unless the customer asks to reset everything. Stop a pending request when the customer says to cancel, but distinguish cancelling the conversation step from cancelling an order.
