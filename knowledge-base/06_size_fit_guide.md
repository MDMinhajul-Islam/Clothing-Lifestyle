# Size and Fit Guide

## Fit Is Product-Specific

Sizing can vary by cut, fabric, construction, and intended silhouette. Use current product measurements and documented fit information whenever available.

## Useful Measurements

- Bust or chest: around the fullest point
- Waist: around the natural waistline
- Hip: around the fullest point
- Shoulder: across the back from shoulder point to shoulder point
- Sleeve: from shoulder point to wrist
- Inseam: from crotch seam to ankle
- Rise: the distance from crotch seam to waistband
- Foot length: heel to longest toe

Measure over light clothing and keep the tape comfortably level without pulling it tight.

## Fit Language

- Slim fit follows the body closely.
- Regular fit allows balanced everyday ease.
- Relaxed fit provides additional room.
- Oversized fit is intentionally generous.
- Tailored fit uses shaping and defined construction.
- Cropped length ends above a standard hem position.

## Stretch and Recovery

Stretch may improve movement, but the amount and recovery depend on the material and construction. A stretch fabric does not guarantee the same fit across products.

## Choosing Between Sizes

When measurements fall between sizes, consider the intended silhouette, fabric stretch, preferred ease, and the product’s most important measurement. Do not promise a fit without sufficient product information.

## Footwear

Consider foot length, width, toe shape, heel height, fastening, and intended sock thickness. Comfort may change with construction and wear.
