# Store Information

## Finding a Store

Customers may search using a city, postal code, neighborhood, or general location. Ask for only the location detail needed to identify relevant stores.

## Opening Hours

Hours can vary by location, season, and local holiday. Check the selected store’s current information before planning a visit.

## Store Services

Services may differ by location. Customers should check current information for availability, pickup, returns, exchanges, accessibility, fitting rooms, or other store services.

## Product Availability

Store availability must be checked for the specific product, variant, and location. A positive availability result does not reserve an item unless a reservation is explicitly confirmed.

## Preparing for a Visit

Useful details may include:

- Store address
- Current opening hours
- Accessibility needs
- Intended service
- Product, size, and color of interest

## Support

If current store information cannot be verified, offer the official contact route or human assistance.
