# Styling Guide

## Shopping by Occasion

### Interview

Aim for polished, comfortable clothing that supports confidence and movement. Ask about the organization, expected formality, climate, and whether the customer prefers tailoring, a dress, or coordinated separates.

### Office

Build around versatile pieces that can be repeated and layered. Consider workplace formality, comfort across the day, and the customer’s preferred level of structure.

### Business Casual

Combine refined and relaxed elements. Clean lines, considered layers, and comfortable materials can create a professional look without full formalwear.

### Formal

Clarify the dress code, venue, time, and weather. Favor a deliberate silhouette and restrained coordination, then add accessories according to the customer’s comfort and taste.

### Wedding

Ask whether the customer is a guest or part of the wedding party. Confirm the dress code, venue, season, and any colors or styles to avoid.

### Cocktail

Look for an elevated silhouette with enough ease for the event. Texture, proportion, footwear, and one focused accessory can make the outfit feel complete.

### Date Night

Start with the setting and the customer’s desired mood. Balance personality, comfort, and confidence rather than assuming a particular level of formality.

### Graduation

Consider the ceremony setting, weather, walking, photography, and any gown worn over the outfit. Choose comfortable proportions that remain polished throughout the day.

### Vacation

Prioritize adaptable pieces that coordinate easily and suit the destination. Ask about climate, activities, luggage space, and preferred coverage.

### Airport

Choose comfort, breathable layers, practical footwear, and easy movement. A useful outfit should adapt to changing temperatures during travel.

### Beach

Consider lightweight layers, sun coverage, comfort, and materials suited to warm conditions. Ask whether the outfit is for swimming, walking, dining, or all-day wear.

### Resort

Combine relaxed shapes with refined details. Plan pieces that can move from daytime activities to evening settings with minimal changes.

### Winter

Build warmth through layers. Consider insulation, wind protection, footwear, and the balance between an outer layer and the outfit underneath.

### Summer

Prioritize breathability, comfortable ease, and sun-aware coverage. Lightweight construction and a simple color story can make warm-weather dressing easier.

### Party

Ask about venue, dress code, movement, and the customer’s preferred focal point. A party look can be expressive without sacrificing comfort.

### Everyday

Focus on repeatable combinations, comfortable fit, practical care, and pieces that work with the customer’s existing wardrobe.

### Smart Casual

Use polished pieces with relaxed styling. The goal is intentional and presentable without the structure of formal dressing.

### Luxury Minimalist

Prioritize clean proportion, considered fabric, restrained color, and subtle detail. Keep the outfit focused and avoid unnecessary elements.

## Workwear

Begin with the workplace and desired formality. Tailored trousers, refined knitwear, shirts, blazers, midi silhouettes, and restrained accessories can create polished combinations. Comfort and movement remain important.

## Weddings and Celebrations

Ask about dress code, venue, time of day, weather, and the customer’s role. Avoid colors or styles the customer wishes to exclude. Consider footwear comfort and layering for temperature changes.

## Evening Dressing

Explore silhouette, texture, and focal detail. A simple outfit can gain impact through proportion, jewelry, footwear, or a distinctive bag.

## Casual Dressing

Balance ease with intentional proportion. Relaxed denim, knitwear, shirts, T-shirts, overshirts, sneakers, and practical accessories can be combined according to fit preference.

## Travel

Prioritize layering, comfort, versatile colors, useful pockets, and pieces that can form several outfits. Consider climate and planned activities.

## Building a Complete Look

A balanced recommendation may include:

1. A main garment or anchor piece
2. A complementary layer
3. Footwear suited to the occasion
4. One practical or expressive accessory

## Proportion

Contrast can create balance: fitted with relaxed, cropped with high-rise, or fluid with structured. These are options rather than rules.

## Personal Style

Respect the customer’s preferred coverage, comfort, identity, and expression. Avoid assumptions based on age, body shape, or gender.
