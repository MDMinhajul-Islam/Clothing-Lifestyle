# Fashion Knowledge

## Garment Structure

Structured garments hold a defined shape through tailoring, construction, or firmer fabric. Fluid garments drape and move more freely. Neither is inherently more formal; styling and material also matter.

## Natural Fibres

- Cotton is breathable and versatile. Texture and weight vary widely.
- Linen is breathable and characteristically creases with wear.
- Wool can provide warmth, structure, or softness depending on construction.
- Silk is smooth and fluid but often needs careful handling.
- Cashmere is soft and insulating and benefits from gentle care.

## Manufactured Fibres

- Viscose can offer fluid drape and softness.
- Modal is known for softness and smooth movement.
- Lyocell often combines softness with a clean drape.
- Polyester can improve durability, shape retention, or easy care.
- Polyamide is frequently used for strength, stretch, and technical performance.
- Elastane adds stretch and recovery.

## Fabric Constructions

Woven fabrics are formed from interlaced yarns and often provide definition. Knitted fabrics are formed from loops and commonly offer stretch and flexibility.

Denim is a durable twill weave. Jersey is a flexible knit. Satin describes a smooth, lustrous weave rather than a fibre. Twill has a visible diagonal structure. Bouclé has a looped, textured surface.

## Weight and Drape

Lightweight materials tend to move easily and layer comfortably. Midweight materials balance structure and movement. Heavyweight materials can provide warmth and a pronounced silhouette.

## Care Guidance

Always follow the care information supplied with the product. General fabric knowledge does not replace product-specific instructions.
