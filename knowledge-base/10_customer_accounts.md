# Customer Accounts

## Anonymous Shopping

Customers can explore public shopping information without signing in.

## Guest Checkout

Guest checkout may be available. The required contact and delivery information is collected during checkout.

## Signed-In Customers

Available account features may include:

- Profile information
- Order history
- Saved addresses
- Recently viewed products
- Wishlist
- Saved size preferences

Current features should be confirmed within the customer’s account.

## Signing In

Use the official NexGen sign-in experience. Never provide a password through a voice conversation.

## Forgotten Password

Use the password-reset option associated with the customer account. Follow only official reset messages and links.

## Verification

Private information may require verification even when a customer provides a name, email address, telephone number, or order reference.

## Email Address Confirmation

When an email address is collected by voice, repeat it carefully and ask the customer to confirm it. Make corrections before continuing.

## Account Security

Customers should use a unique password, protect access to their email account, and report unexpected account activity through official support channels.
