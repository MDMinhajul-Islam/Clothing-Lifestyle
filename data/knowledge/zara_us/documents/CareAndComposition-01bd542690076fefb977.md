# COMPOSITION AND CARE

At Zara, you will find a wide range of products made of natural, artificial and synthetic materials. You can find detailed information about the composition of each item at both Zara.com and in our traditional stores.
Our Sustainability Policy also includes the obligation that all our products of animal origin come from animals that are treated ethically and responsibly, and in no case from animals slaughtered exclusively for the sale of their skins, shells, feathers or down, among others.

We are working with monitoring programs to guarantee compliance with the social, environmental, and health and safety standards of our garments.
We have a traceability system that allows us to monitor the supply chain of our productions. We ask our suppliers to inform us about all the facilities involved in the production processes, from the yarn or fiber to the final garment. This includes both own and external factories, as well as the intermediaries involved in each process. To assess its compliance, we have developed an audit program and plans for continual improvement.

Please visit our Join Life section for more information.
CARE RECOMMENDATIONS

For each product, both on the website as well as on our garment labels, you will find information about the best way to care for your garments.

Also, we created a guide that will help you care for your garments and lengthen their useful life. Visit Care for your clothing for more information.
