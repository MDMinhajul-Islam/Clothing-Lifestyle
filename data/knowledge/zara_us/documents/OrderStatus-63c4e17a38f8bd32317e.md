# STATUS OF MY ONLINE ORDER

You can track your order through your account in the Purchases section. If you are not a registered user, you will have this information by clicking the "Manage Your Order" link in any of your order or shipment confirmation emails.

Your order will go through these steps:
* Purchased: you have completed your purchase correctly.
* Being processed: we received your order information.
* Being prepared: your order is being prepared in the warehouse.
* Shipped from the warehouse: the order has already left our facilities.
* Out with the courier: your order is on its way, you will receive it shortly.
* Delivered to the customer: your order was delivered.

Remember you will receive your order updates by email.
## Frequently Asked Questions

### WHEN WILL I RECEIVE MY ORDER?

When placing the order, we will provide you with the estimated delivery date. Then you can track it from your account or, if you purchased as a guest, from the link in any of the emails we have sent you about your purchase.
