# HOW TO MAKE AN EXCHANGE

At Zara we give you the option of changing items from your purchase for free. We will explain the conditions to you and how to handle it.

### CONDITIONS TO COMPLETE AN EXCHANGE

* The exchange must be handled within the established period:
Online purchase: you have 30 days from the shipment date of your order.
Purchase at a store: you have 30 days from the purchase date.
* The items need to be in their original condition and with all their labels.
* You must make the exchange in the same market/region where you made the purchase.
You can request an exchange online for items from MASSIMO DUTTI. Exchanges cannot be made for items from HOME.

You can find additional information about the Exchange and Return Policy or your right to withdrawal in our Terms and Conditions .
### EXCHANGE IN A ZARA STORE

If you have purchased an item in-store or online, we give you the option of exchanging it for another size or color at any of our stores.

To exchange an online order, you do not have to request a return beforehand.

Locate the store nearest you using our store locator .
### ONLINE EXCHANGE

If you purchased an item online, you have the option of exchanging a size or color through our website or app.

The process is very easy: just select the items to exchange and the size or color you would like to receive. The selected items will be sent in a new order and you will have to return those you no longer want. We offer various options that you can consult in the How to make a return section.
The return period for the merchandise you do not want is 14 days from the exchange request. If this period has passed and we have not received the items indicated to be exchanged, you will be charged the amount of the new items via the payment method used for the purchase.

If you want to modify or cancel your exchange request, please contact us so we can help manage this process.
## Frequently Asked Questions
### WHEN CAN I NOT REQUEST AN ONLINE EXCHANGE?

In the situations listed below, it is not possible to request an online exchange, but you can always visit one of our stores to complete the process.
* The online exchange option is not available for orders paid via: Paypal, Ideal, Bancontact, Diners Club, P24, gift card, ApplePay and AndroidPay (some of these payment methods may not be available in your market/region).
* The exchange option will not be available if the item purchased is one size fits all or if it is no longer available.
* Online exchanges are not available for purchases that include a gift receipt. You always have the option to make an exchange in-store or a return.
* Only two exchange requests can be made at a time, with a maximum of 5 items per request.
