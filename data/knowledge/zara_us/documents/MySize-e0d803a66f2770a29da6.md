# WHAT'S MY SIZE?

At Zara we offer a wide range of sizes that can vary according to the style, cut and fabric of each garment. So you can select the size that will best fit you, we have two tools for you to use, our measurement guide and the Find your size option.

MEASUREMENT GUIDE

You can find our measurement guide on the product page. Select the size you want and we will show you what body measurements it is tailored to.

FIND YOUR SIZE
To assist you in your size selection, we suggest you use the Find your size option, available on each item’s page. We will calculate which size is best for you through this tool, based on information you provide (your body measurements and how you like your clothes to fit).
## Frequently Asked Questions

### HOW CAN I CHECK WHAT AN ITEM’S MEASUREMENTS ARE?

If you want to know the measurements of any item you can check our measurement guide. If you do not find the measurement you need, contact us so we can help you.
