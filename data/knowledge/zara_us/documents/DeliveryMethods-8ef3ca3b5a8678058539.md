# SHIPPING METHODS, TIMES AND PRICING

The shipping options may vary based on the delivery address, the time you place your order, and availability of items.

At the time of processing your order, we will show you the shipping methods available, the charges and the estimated delivery date.

Please remember that deliveries are only made on business days.

Remember that the delivery address of your order has to be in the same market/region where you made the purchase.
### SHIPMENT TO A ZARA STORE

Time: 3-5 working days

Cost: FREE

At the time of placing the order we will show you the stores available and the estimated delivery date.

You have 15 days to pick up the order at the store. You only need to present your ZARA QR or the QR code from the electronic receipt. If someone else comes in your place, they must also show their identification card.

Use our store locator to find your nearest Zara store.
This option is not available for orders that include a large item from our HOME collection.
### HOME DELIVERY

* Next day delivery - $ 14.95 or $ 19.95 (depending on delivery address). $ 25.00 only for Alaska and Hawaii.
* Delivery in 2-4 working days - $ 4.95

Please keep in mind if your order includes a large item from HOME, corresponding shipping costs will be charged.

If you made your purchase as a registered user, you can change the delivery address by accessing your order details in your account ; should it still be possible per the status of your order.
### SHIPMENT TO A DELIVERY POINT

Period: 2-4 working days

Cost: $ 4.95

When you complete your purchase we will show you the available delivery points and the estimated date you will receive your order.

We will let you know when your order is available at the selected delivery point. Remember that you will need to provide identification to be able to pick it up. If another person comes in your place, they will need to present written authorization and a copy of your identification document.
This option is not available for orders that include a large item from our HOME collection.
## Frequently Asked Questions

### WHEN WILL I RECEIVE MY ORDER?

When placing the order, we will provide you with the estimated delivery date. Then you can track it from your account or, if you purchased as a guest, from the link in any of the emails we have sent you about your purchase.

### CAN I CHANGE THE SHIPPING METHOD?

If the purchase has been finalized, the selected shipping method cannot be changed.
### WHAT HAPPENS IF MY ORDER COULD NOT BE DELIVERED?

If you have selected home delivery and you are absent at the time of delivery, we suggest that you contact us and we will revise it with the shipping company.

### CAN I DECLINE DELIVERY OF MY ORDER?

If you no longer want to receive your purchase, we suggest you contact us so we can manage it.
### CAN I DELAY MY ORDER?

If we were not able to deliver your order on the estimated date, we will send you an email and you can see the new delivery date in the order detail.
### MY ORDER SHOWS AS BEING DELIVERED BUT I HAVE NOT RECEIVED IT. WHAT CAN I DO?

Sometimes a delivered package can be misplaced or received by someone else at your address. Please follow these steps to help locate your order:
* Wait 72 hours: Occasionally, couriers mark packages as delivered a bit early. Please wait 72 hours from the ‘delivered’ status before reporting it as missing.
* Check around and track your order: Look in all possible delivery locations, including mailrooms and with neighbors or building staff who might have accepted it on your behalf. Also, we recommend you to use the tracking number found in your shipping confirmation email or in your account order details. You can often find the courier’s proof of delivery there too.
* Contact the courier: In many cases, the courier can provide more details or help locate the package.
* Still missing? If you can’t locate your package after 72 hours and the courier is unable to help, please contact our customer service team.
### I BOUGHT A LARGE ITEM FROM ZARA HOME. HOW WILL IT BE DELIVERED?

The product will remain packed until it is delivered to the room of your choice.

We will contact you to check the measurements of the delivery space and inform you of the dimensions of the packaging of these items.

In the event of a crane service being necessary to make the delivery, we will assist you with its managing according to its availability. The cost will not be included in the shipping costs applied during the purchase.
