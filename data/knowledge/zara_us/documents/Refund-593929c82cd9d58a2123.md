# RETURN REFUND

Once we have handled the return of your items, we will continue with the refund.

REFUND OF IN-STORE PURCHASE

If you have returned an item purchased in-store, the refund will be made with the same method of payment used for the purchase.

If a refund to the original method of payment is not possible, our store staff will indicate an alternative method.

REIMBURSEMENT OF AN ONLINE PURCHASE
When you make a return of an online purchase, you will receive the refund via the same payment method you used for the purchase.

If you sent your return through a shipping company, we will process it in the 72 hours after receiving it in our facilities.

Once the return has been completed we will send you a confirmation email.

Keep in mind that once the return has been made, you will receive the amount within a maximum period of 14 days, depending on your bank.
If you do not see a credit to your account within 14 days, please use this confirmation and present it to your banking institution so they may help you facilitate the process.

You can find additional information about the Exchange and Return Policy or your right to withdrawal in our Terms and Conditions .
## Frequently Asked Questions

### WHY HAVE I NOT RECEIVED MY REFUND CONFIRMATION EMAIL?

If you have not received the email with the information from your refund, please check that the email address associated with your account is correct.

Keep in mind that if you make a return through a delivery point, it will be processed once the items arrive at our facilities. Once completed, you will receive an email with the return information.
### I NO LONGER HAVE THE CARD I USED TO MAKE THE PURCHASE. HOW CAN I RECEIVE MY REFUND?

The refund will always be handled via same payment method used for the order. If you do not have the card used for the purchase available we suggest you contact your bank so they can assist you.
### I NO LONGER HAVE THE GIFT CARD I USED TO PAY. HOW WILL I RECEIVE MY REFUND?

If the return is for an online purchase, together with the confirmation email for the return, you will receive a virtual copy of that card with the updated balance.

If you make the return from a purchase made in-store, our store staff will issue the refund on a Gift Card or on any other Gift Card you may have.
### WHY DID I RECEIVE A PARTIAL REFUND FROM MY RETURN?

Keep in mind that, should you send us your items in different packages, we may issue the refund at different times. We will send you an email confirmation with the detail of each one.

If you prefer, you can contact us so we can review your case in detail.
### HOW CAN I GET THE INVOICE FOR MY REFUND?

You can access your reimbursement invoice through your order detail. If you made the purchase without registering, you can do it from the link found in your return confirmation email.
