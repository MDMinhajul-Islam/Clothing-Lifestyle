## Frequently Asked Questions

### WHEN WILL I BE CHARGED FOR MY ORDER?

The charge will take place once your order is shipped. If you paid with Paypal or a Gift Card, it will go through immediately once the purchase is completed.

### HOW CAN I PAY WITH A GIFT CARD?

If you received a Gift Card from us from a return with the amount of the refund, you can use it to place an order. You have to select “Gift Card” as the method of payment and enter the corresponding numbers.
### CAN I APPLY A DISCOUNT CODE TO MY PURCHASE?

At Zara we don’t have discount codes. Subscribe to our Newsletter to stay up to date on all our promotions.

### CAN I PAY FOR MY PURCHASES IN INSTALLMENTS?

Payment terms depend on the conditions associated with the card you used to make the purchase.
### I HAVE BEEN CHARGED TWICE FOR THE PURCHASE AMOUNT. WHAT CAN I DO?

Some banking institutions may withhold a pre-authorization and an actual charge. This amount will automatically be deleted. If that is not the case, we recommend that you contact your bank so they may expedite removal of the pre-authorization withholding amount.
### HOW DO I PAY FOR AN ONLINE ORDER MADE FROM A STORE?

At the time of purchase our store staff will inform you of the available options in that store to pay for your order:

* From the device you used to make the purchase.
* At the store register. You have two hours to make the payment. If you do not pay at the register, the order will be canceled.

Keep in mind that the availability of these options can vary depending on the Zara store you visit.
